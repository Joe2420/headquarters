# Audio Bible v1

    **Owner:** Audio Design  
    **Status:** Engineering Blueprint  
    **Baseline:** HDR v1.0 Design Freeze  
    **Purpose:** Convert the locked Headquarters design into buildable implementation guidance.

    ---


Audio philosophy:
- No notification addiction.
- No arcade effects.
- No profit celebration.
- Mechanical, restrained, institutional sounds only.

Initial sound families:
- hydraulic_door_open
- hydraulic_door_close
- low_hq_hum
- radar_sweep_soft
- authorization_stamp
- archive_stamp
- vault_lock
- guardian_bell
- institutional_bell
- printer_mechanical
- boot_relay_sequence
- shutdown_power_down

Every sound must have a purpose, trigger, priority, and silence fallback.


    ---

    ## Acceptance Criteria

    - The implementation can be traced back to HDR v1.0.
    - No new product philosophy is introduced here.
    - The document gives Codex/developers actionable engineering direction.
    - Any unresolved item is marked as an implementation decision, not hidden.
