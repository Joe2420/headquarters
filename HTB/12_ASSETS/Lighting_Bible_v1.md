# Lighting Bible v1

    **Owner:** Visual Design  
    **Status:** Engineering Blueprint  
    **Baseline:** HDR v1.0 Design Freeze  
    **Purpose:** Convert the locked Headquarters design into buildable implementation guidance.

    ---


Lighting profiles:

- Command Center: cold white, restrained.
- Ready Room: warm, safe, focused.
- Observation Room: cool blue, low stimulation.
- War Room: focused amber/blue, mission intensity.
- Guardian Wing: dark graphite, deep green, protective.
- Archives: warm low light, institutional memory.
- Internal Affairs: clean white, evidence-focused.
- Recovery: warm gold, low contrast.
- Emergency/Guardian Lock: reduced panels, controlled red/black.

Lighting communicates operational state. It is never decorative.


    ---

    ## Acceptance Criteria

    - The implementation can be traced back to HDR v1.0.
    - No new product philosophy is introduced here.
    - The document gives Codex/developers actionable engineering direction.
    - Any unresolved item is marked as an implementation decision, not hidden.
