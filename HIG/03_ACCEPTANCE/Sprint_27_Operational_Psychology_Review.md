# Sprint 27 - Operational Psychology Review

## Scope

Sprint 27 added deterministic operational psychology cues to the existing Headquarters desktop experience.

This sprint did not add AI, network calls, database schema changes, backend rewrites, mission lifecycle rewrites, or new room architecture.

## Room Mindset Engine

Each room now exposes an operational mindset profile:

- Ready Room: preparation
- Observation: patience and evidence
- War Room: decision and responsibility
- Debrief: reflection
- Archive: closure and permanence
- Journal: record
- Doctrine: law
- Academy: growth
- Guardian: security
- Intelligence: analysis

## Commander Pacing

Commander can derive room-specific pacing lines from the active psychology profile.

Pacing remains deterministic:

- Observation is slow.
- War Room is direct.
- Debrief is reflective.
- Archive and Doctrine are formal.

## Psychological Friction

The psychology profile marks important actions that require deliberate confirmation:

- Authorization
- Mission completion/debrief
- Archive sealing
- Doctrine promotion

No new mutation behavior was added.

## Mission Ceremonies

Mission ceremony copy now incorporates psychology cues where available, reinforcing mission acceptance, observation completion, authorization, return, and archive.

## Focus Mode

Guided rooms now show a concise operational mindset strip so the operator sees the intended mental posture without opening another panel.

## Environmental Reinforcement

The Situation Board now includes an Operational Mindset surface alongside operational awareness.

## Operator Presence

Psychology copy addresses the operator as physically present in Headquarters:

- Remain in Observation.
- Stay in the command seat.
- Return to the Debrief Theater.
- Stand before the Archive vault.

## Long Session Awareness

The deterministic psychology model supports long-session awareness for future timer integration.

## Remaining Gaps

- No live elapsed-session timer is connected yet.
- Deliberate confirmation markers are descriptive only until future ceremony/confirmation tasks.
- Further animation and audio reinforcement remain future work.

## Sprint 28 Recommendation

Sprint 28 should focus on Living Headquarters OS: command chair operating console, broadcast feed, operational notifications, and dynamic situation board refinement.
