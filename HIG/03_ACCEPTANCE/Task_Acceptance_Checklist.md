# Task Acceptance Checklist

Before approving a Codex task, verify:

## Scope Control

- The task did not expand beyond its stated scope.
- No unrelated features were added.
- No new architecture was invented without approval.

## Build Health

- Install/build/typecheck commands succeed or documented blockers are clear.
- Tests exist where appropriate.
- No broken imports.

## Repository Health

- Files are placed in the correct packages/folders.
- Names follow the repository conventions.
- Public exports are intentional.

## Headquarters Identity

- No market prediction was added.
- No social/gamified mechanics were added.
- Operator responsibility remains intact.
- Language remains institutional and calm.

## Approval Decision

Choose one:

- Approved.
- Needs revision.
- Re-scope.
- Reject.
