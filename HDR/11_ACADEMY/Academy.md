# Academy

The Academy turns weaknesses into training.

It teaches professional conduct, not strategies.

Courses may include:
- Waiting Under Uncertainty
- Recovering After Losses
- Protecting Winning Days
- Mission Authorization
- Honest Debrief Writing
- Negotiation Language Detection
- Capital Stewardship
- Observation Discipline
- Return to Base

Completion is earned through repeated behavior, not quizzes alone.
