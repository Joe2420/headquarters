# PH1-0013 — SOP-013 Re-entry Protocol

**Recovery Phase:** Phase 1.0–2.0  
**Category:** SOP  
**Priority:** CORE  
**Status:** SPECIFIED  
**Owner:** Headquarters Architecture

## Purpose
Prevent repeated re-entry into the same move after closing.

## Operator Experience
Requires new structure, new authorization, and waiting period of 2–5 candles.

## Engineering Implementation
Track exits and prevent immediate re-entry unless new authorization passes.
