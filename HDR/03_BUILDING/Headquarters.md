# Headquarters Building

Headquarters is not a menu. It is a digital institution represented as a secure facility.

Movement through rooms reinforces psychological transitions: civilian life to operator state, observation to authorization, deployment to return, debrief to archive, recovery to renewed command.

## Core Feeling
Heavy. Calm. Secure. Quiet. Institutional. Professional.

## Levels
- Level 0 — Arrival: checkpoint, blast doors, transition corridor, entrance hall.
- Level 1 — Operations: Command Center, Ready Room, Observation Room, War Room, Guardian Wing.
- Level 2 — Institution: Archives, Academy, Engineering, Internal Affairs, Medical, Recognition.
- Level 3 — Strategic: Founder's Office, Observatory, Council Chamber, Legacy Vault, Constitution Hall.
