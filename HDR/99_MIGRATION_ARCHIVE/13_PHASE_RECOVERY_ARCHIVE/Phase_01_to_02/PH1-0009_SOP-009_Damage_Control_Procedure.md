# PH1-0009 — SOP-009 Damage Control Procedure

**Recovery Phase:** Phase 1.0–2.0  
**Category:** SOP  
**Priority:** CORE  
**Status:** SPECIFIED

## Purpose
Escalate restrictions after losses before cascades form.

## Operator Experience
After 1 loss slow down; after 2 losses A+ only; after 3 losses suspend mission and cooldown.

## Engineering Implementation
Threat engine escalates and alters authorization criteria.
