# PH2-0007 — Secure Door Transition

**Recovery Phase:** Phase 1.3–2.0  
**Category:** Environment  
**Priority:** IMPORTANT  
**Status:** SPECIFIED  
**Owner:** Headquarters Architecture

## Purpose
Use short door/elevator transitions between major rooms to reinforce state changes.

## Operator Experience
Operator feels movement deeper into or out of operations.

## Engineering Implementation
Animated transitions 700–1200ms, skippable in accessibility mode.

## Dependencies
Animation Bible; Audio
