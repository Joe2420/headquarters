# Phase 0.1–1.0 Recovery Summary

This phase defines Headquarters as a Decision Operating System and Chief Risk Officer, not a trade journal. The recovered items focus on the foundational mission, the primary metrics, and the mission lifecycle.

Key recoveries include Operational Readiness Score, Mission Integrity, Threat Level, Execution XP, Account Killer Score, Edge Quality Score, Operation Codenames, Outcome vs Execution separation, and Mission History as the primary archive unit.
