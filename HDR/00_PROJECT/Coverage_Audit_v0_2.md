## Remaining known limitations

- This audit cannot guarantee 100% recovery of every phrase or micro-idea from the full conversation because earlier context may be unavailable.
- v0.2 should be treated as the new canonical baseline.
- Future sessions must update registry files directly instead of relying on chat memory.
