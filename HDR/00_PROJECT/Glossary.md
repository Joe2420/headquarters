# Glossary

- Operator: The trader reporting to Headquarters.
- Professional Joe: The disciplined identity that should remain in command.
- Civilian Joe: The non-operational identity outside Headquarters.
- Headquarters: The institution/environment that prepares, protects, teaches, and remembers.
- Mission: A trading day or operational session.
- Operation: A specific authorized capital deployment.
- Campaign: A multi-week or multi-month improvement objective.
- Deployment: External trade execution on the broker/platform.
- Guardian Protocol: Funded/capital-protection operating state.
- Archives: SQLite-backed institutional memory.
- Doctrine: Evidence-tested operational rules.
- Command Chair: Symbolic control state identifying who currently holds command.
- Return to Base: End the session after objective completion or risk threshold.
