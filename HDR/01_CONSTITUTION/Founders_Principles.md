# Founder's Principles

The Founder represents the original intent of Headquarters.

The Founder never participates in daily operations and never appears as an AI personality. The Founder exists through the Founder's Office, Founder's Journal, handwritten questions, the Constitution, and permanent architectural decisions.

## Founder Test

Before adding a major feature, ask:

> If Headquarters existed fifty years ago, would this feature still make sense?

If not, reject it.

## Founder's Letter

> One day, you will realize that the market was never your greatest opponent. The greatest battle was fought every morning before the first trade. If Headquarters helped you win that battle, then every line of code written within these walls was worthwhile.
