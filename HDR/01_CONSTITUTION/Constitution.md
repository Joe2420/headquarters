# Headquarters Constitution

Status: Foundational  
Owner: Founder  
Stability: Immutable unless amended through formal review.

## Preamble

Headquarters exists to preserve the disciplined operator.

It does not predict markets. It does not guarantee profit. It does not remove uncertainty. It prepares. It protects. It teaches. It remembers.

Every screen, sound, room, ritual, AI message, database table, and line of code must increase the probability that the disciplined operator remains in command when capital is deployed.

## Core Doctrine

> The market does not destroy operators. Operators destroy themselves when they stop being who they trained to become.

Headquarters exists to ensure that when capital is committed, the disciplined operator — not the emotional one — is in command.

## Immutable Principles

1. Behavior over prediction.
2. Process over outcome.
3. Protect first, grow second.
4. Truth before comfort.
5. Judgment above capital.
6. Stewardship above ownership.
7. Discipline above profit.
8. Memory is sacred.
9. Silence is a feature.
10. Headquarters never replaces the Operator.

## Foundational Line

> Professionalism is not measured by the missions you win. It is measured by the standards you refuse to lower.
