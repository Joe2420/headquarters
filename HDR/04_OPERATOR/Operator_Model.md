# Operator Model

The Operator Model is the internal representation of Joe's operational state.

Tracked dimensions:
- Professional Alignment
- Identity Drift
- Patience
- Judgment Reserve
- Decision Fatigue
- Mental Noise
- Operational Load
- Confidence Calibration
- Recovery Quality
- Stewardship
- Command Authority
- Trust
- Mission Momentum

The model never predicts the market. It models the operator.
