# COG-0002 — Council Deliberation Delay

**Status:** Registered  
**Priority:** IMPORTANT  
**Category:** AI / Interaction

## Purpose
Authorization may pause briefly to feel like institutional deliberation, not loading or instant AI output.

## Scope
This specification captures the concept as part of the Headquarters Design Repository v0.3 expansion. It should be expanded during the relevant volume pass before implementation.

## Dependencies

## User Experience
The feature must preserve the Headquarters identity: calm, professional, evidence-based, and behavior-first. It must not introduce noise, ego, prediction, social pressure, or dopamine-driven engagement.
