# Behavioral Black Box

Purpose: reconstruct the chain of behaviors leading to mission compromise or excellence.

It asks:
> When did the mission stop being recoverable?

Tracks:
- skipped briefing
- checklist changes
- rapid chart switching
- mouse tempo
- denied authorization
- override request
- revenge sequence
- stop movement
- mission fatigue
- rule negotiation language

It identifies chains of failure, chains of excellence, butterfly moments, point of no return, and precursor sequences.
